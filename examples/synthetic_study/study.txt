Study design:
10 adults took part in a 7-day ambulatory study. Each wore a wrist sensor
that records acceleration (ENMO, in milli-g) and optical heart rate in
1-minute epochs, and carried a smartphone EMA app. The app sent 5 random
prompts a day between 08:00 and 22:00 asking "What were you doing just
before this survey?", and participants could also log exercise themselves.

Scientific question:
Are EMA-reported exercise episodes associated with higher wrist activity
than EMA-reported rest episodes?

Available files:
- sensor.csv: participant_id, timestamp, enmo_mg, heart_rate_bpm
- ema.csv: participant_id, timestamp, prompt_type (random or self-initiated),
  activity (the answer; empty when a prompt was missed)
- pipeline.py: current analysis pipeline

The current pipeline reports almost no exercise-versus-rest difference.
